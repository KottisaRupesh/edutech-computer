Exam Guide

Do's
  • Login 10 minutes before exam starts
  • Ensure stable internet connection
  • Keep your ID proof ready
  • Read each question carefully
  • Use a quiet, distraction-free environment
  • Submit the exam before time ends
Don'ts
  • Don't refresh the exam page
  • Don't switch tabs during the exam
  • Don't use external help or materials
  • Don't share your login credentials
  • Don't leave the exam unattended
  • Don't use multiple devices
